About me

Engineering that moves data into action.

I’m Muhammad Mudassir Raza, a Senior Data Engineer who builds and operates end-to-end data pipelines for analytics, marketing and revenue teams. My day-to-day work covers ingesting data from third-party APIs, modeling it in warehouses such as BigQuery and Snowflake, and keeping it accurate with scheduled, self-healing jobs.

I care about the unglamorous details that make pipelines trustworthy: incremental loading, delete-then-append reconciliation, retries with exponential backoff, deduplication and Slack alerting, so a failed run gets noticed immediately and a re-run never creates duplicates.

Beyond batch pipelines, I work with Kafka and PySpark for streaming, Airflow for orchestration, and LLM / n8n automation to turn data into commentary, assistants and workflows. I’m certified across AWS, Databricks and Python, and was recognised in the NASA Astronauts Hackathon.
